## Why Plex

<ul class="plex-features not-content">
    <li>
        <h3>Everyone gets OP</h3>
        <p>And the server stays standing. Protect spawn with one preset, turn off explosions, fluid spread, or redstone with one command, and purge mobs before they cause lag.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h3>Griefers, handled</h3>
        <p>Ban, mute, freeze, or smite a player in seconds. Bans cover names and IPs, and every punishment stays on record with your notes beside it.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h3>Undo the damage</h3>
        <p>Plex works with Oasis, a powerful rollback plugin built for anarchy and free-OP servers. When you ban a griefer, Plex can roll back their last day of changes.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h3>Network-wide bans</h3>
        <p>Run more than one server? With Redis, a ban on one server applies on all of them, and your admins share one admin chat.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h3>Only what you need</h3>
        <p>Modules add a web dashboard, guilds, disguises, and more. Install the ones your server uses and skip the rest.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h3>Switching from TFM?</h3>
        <p>Bring your TotalFreedomMod indefinite bans with you. IBConverter moves them into Plex, so you do not start over.</p>
    </li>
</ul>
